How Mike works

Mike understands what's happening on your computer, and helps you act on it — not just talk about it.

Talk

Speak naturally, or type. No commands to memorize, no syntax to learn — just say what you need.

See

Mike understands what's on your screen, in your files, and in your open apps, so you don't have to explain the context yourself.

Act

It can use applications, move and edit files, and carry out real tasks on your computer — then tell you exactly what changed.

Remember

Mike keeps track of what matters so you don't have to explain yourself twice, session after session.

Built around your privacy, honestly explained

Mike is local-first: designed to keep as much of your activity and data on your own computer as possible, and to be plain with you about what leaves it and why.

That's a design principle, not an absolute guarantee — no assistant built on AI models can promise perfect privacy. Mike will always tell you what it can see, what it remembers, and how to control it.
