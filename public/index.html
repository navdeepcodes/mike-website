What Mike does

One assistant for everything on your computer.

Mike doesn't just talk about your work — it works with the files, apps and projects you already have.

Your apps and screen

Opens the apps you already use and works them the way you would — clicking, typing, and reading what's in a window.

Your files

Finds files by name, reads PDFs, Word and PowerPoint, and writes and edits documents and spreadsheets — formulas included.

Studying

Explains step by step, works maths out exactly instead of guessing, and turns your notes and slides into summaries.

Code

Works inside your projects: reads and edits code, runs commands, checks what broke, and connects to VS Code.

Voice

Say “Hey Mike”, or press Ctrl Shift Space from anywhere. Mike listens — and can answer out loud.

Memory

Remembers what you ask it to — your courses, your deadlines, how you like things explained — and forgets on request.

You stay in charge

Mike asks before anything it can't take back.

  • Deleting, overwriting, sending — each waits for your OK, in plain words.
  • Every change is recorded in Activity, and changes to files can be undone.
  • Switch abilities off — web, email, commands, files — in Settings → Permissions.
  • Stop Mike any time with Esc.

Privacy

Private by design, and plain about it.

Thinks on your computer

Mike's AI model runs on your own machine through Ollama. Your conversations, files, screen and voice are processed there — not on our servers.

No tracking

No analytics, no telemetry, no ads. We don't receive your conversations or how you use Mike.

An account is optional

Only your email, name and photo — so Mike knows you on another computer. Delete it any time, in one click.

The in-browser preview is different: it runs on a cloud model, so what you type there is sent to NVIDIA. Read the Privacy Policy
