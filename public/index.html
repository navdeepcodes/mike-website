Talk to him from any app

Say “Hey Mike”, or press Ctrl Shift Space wherever you are. He listens, does it, and can answer out loud.

Documents and PDFs

Privacy

Private by design, and plain about it.

Thinks on your computer

Mike's model runs on your own PC through Ollama, so your chats, files, screen and voice stay there. Turn on Fast mode and your own free Cloudflare account answers instead — screenshots never leave either way.

No tracking

No analytics, no telemetry, no ads. We don't receive your conversations or how you use Mike.

An account is optional

Only your email, name and photo — so Mike knows you on another computer. Delete it any time.

The in-browser preview runs on a cloud model, so what you type there is sent to Cloudflare Workers AI (with NVIDIA as backup). Privacy Policy
