You stay in charge

Mike asks before anything it can't take back.

  • Deleting, overwriting, sending — each waits for your OK, in plain words.
  • Every change is recorded in Activity, and changes to files can be undone.
  • Switch abilities off — web, email, commands, files — in Settings → Permissions.
  • Stop Mike any time with Esc.

Privacy

Private by design, and plain about it.

Thinks on your computer

Mike's AI model runs on your own machine through Ollama. Your conversations, files, screen and voice are processed there — not on our servers.

No tracking

No analytics, no telemetry, no ads. We don't receive your conversations or how you use Mike.

An account is optional

Only your email, name and photo — so Mike knows you on another computer. Delete it any time, in one click.

The in-browser preview is different: it runs on a cloud model, so what you type there is sent to Cloudflare Workers AI (with NVIDIA as backup). Read the Privacy Policy
